What is SKConnect?

A single platform that connects the Sangguniang Kabataan and the youth of the barangay, so information, services, and projects are easy to reach and open to everyone.

Accessible Information

Announcements, programs, and opportunities are all in one place, not buried in social media posts.

Easy Services

Check requirements, submit documents online, and track your application without visiting the barangay hall.

Transparent Governance

See completed projects, their budgets, and breakdowns so everyone knows where SK funds go.
