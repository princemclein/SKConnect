Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SKConnect.

SKConnect is the web platform of the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita, Olongapo City. It puts SK announcements, opportunities, completed projects, and budget information in one place, so the youth and the community can find them easily.

No. Anyone can browse SKConnect for free without signing up. The only login is the Admin Login, which is for authorized SK officials who manage the announcements and project records.
