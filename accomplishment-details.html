Interpurok-Basketball-League

A youth sports initiative organized by the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita to promote sportsmanship, teamwork, and community participation through an inter-purok basketball competition.

Project Overview

Bring Puroks Together Through Sports

The Inter-Purok Basketball League 2025 was organized by the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) of Barangay Sta. Rita to promote sportsmanship, teamwork, and unity among the youth. Each purok formed a team of 15 players aged 15 to 30, encouraging friendly competition and active community participation.

The league officially opened on October 12, 2025, featuring a parade, the Mx. Interpurok 2025 competition, and the tournament bracket draw. The season concluded on December 7, 2025, with Purok 6E crowned champion, Purok 3C, Purok 6A, and Purok 1B. Romeo Jerald B. Oberez was named Season MVP, while Art Sunga received the Finals MVP award.

Beyond the competition, the league provided a platform for young athletes to showcase their talents, develop discipline and leadership, and build stronger relationships across different puroks. The initiative reflects the SK's commitment to youth development and a more active, united community through sports.

Clean-Up-Drive

A community clean-up drive led by the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita, held in waves to keep the barangay clean and safe.

Project Overview

Clean streets, shared effort

The Clean-Up Drive is a community initiative led by the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita to maintain a clean, safe, and healthy environment through organized clean-up activities. Led by SK Chairperson Hon. Precious Niña Delos Reyes and Environment and Agriculture Committee Chairperson Hon. Chzeniel Ann C. Isip, the project brings together SK officials and youth volunteers to promote environmental awareness and community responsibility.

The first wave, held on September 19, 2026, along Tabacuhan Road, supported Olongapo City's Citywide Coastal Clean-Up Drive. The second wave, conducted on October 10, 2026, was part of the DILG's Barangay at Kalinisan Day (BarKaDa) under the Kalinisan sa Bagong Pilipinas Program, carrying the theme "Bagong Bayanihan ng Mamamayan." Joined by Punong Barangay Hon. Rowena V. Cerezo and Barangay Kagawad Hon. Jun Legaspi, the activity highlighted the importance of bayanihan in building a cleaner and healthier community.

Ikatlong Yugto ng Balagtasan

A youth debate contest on Sipag o Talino for secondary and college students, organized by the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita.

Project Overview

Talino o sipag, sino ang mas matimbang?

Ikatlong Yugto ng Balagtasan is a youth debate contest of the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Sta. Rita, held under the theme "Boses ng Kabataan: Boses ng Bayan". It was part of the Buwan ng Kabataan celebration, and was hosted by Julius "Yoy" Abejo and Arman P. Caseja, together with the whole SK council.

Teams of three argued which matters more in life, Talino (intelligence) or Sipag (hard work), in the Filipino tradition of Balagtasan. As the SK put it, "Talino ang utak ng balangkas ng paggawa, at Sipag ang bisig ng planong binabadya." Twelve groups took the stage. By the end, the debaters showed that both are equally valuable and of little use without each other.
